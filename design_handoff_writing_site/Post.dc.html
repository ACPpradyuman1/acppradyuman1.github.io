Body paragraphs sit at a 62-character measure, which is where serif text at this size stops tiring the eye. Line height is loose enough for long arguments and tight enough that a screen holds a complete thought.

Section heading

Mono section headings keep the hierarchy visible without competing with the serif titles. One level of heading is usually enough; two is the limit before a piece needs to become two pieces.

Where this breaks

Every thesis gets a section for the case against it. Lists here use mono markers so they read as enumerated reasoning rather than bullets.

01 The first risk, stated plainly enough that a reader can disagree with it.
02 The second, with the condition that would change your mind.
03 The third, usually the one that actually matters.

A closing paragraph returns to the claim and says what you would need to see next. No summary of what was just read.
