Every teardown starts the same way: read the filings before reading the takes. What follows is the shape a teardown should take, not a specific company's numbers.

## the business

Start with how the business actually makes money, in one paragraph, before touching valuation. If that paragraph is hard to write, that's the finding.

## where the argument breaks

The bear case is usually simpler than the bull case, and that asymmetry is itself informative.
